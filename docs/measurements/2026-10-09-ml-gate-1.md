## What D1 changed

- Humanoid pedestrian (head, torso, swinging arms and legs, shoes), cyclist (bicycle, pedalling rider) and motorcycle with rider replace the scaled vehicle boxes (`figures.ts`).
- Vehicles: sedan side-profile glasshouse, tinted glass, dark tyres with hubs and wheel arches, bumpers, lamps, plates, distinct truck (cab + cargo box) and bus (window band) silhouettes, deterministic per-id paint (`vehicles.ts`, `meshKit.ts`).
- Lighting: midday ambient 2.7 -> 2.1 and key 1.55 -> 2.3 (other presets moved the same way), plus a fake-AO floor of 0.8.
- Native 640x640 detector frame, fovY = 75.707 deg (the old horizontal FOV); `MOUNT_PITCH_RAD` re-derived and unchanged; `contract/mount_pitch_rad.json` now pins `detector_frame`.
- A defect found on the way: the detector camera's far plane (400 m) was inside the sky dome (radius 900 m), so every detector frame had a black sky. Far plane is now 1400 m.

## Evidence that realism moved the detector, and how far

Same scoring, same int8 model, threshold 0.50. Dev captures used seeds 41-43; benchmark seeds 51-53 and 61-62 were not used for tuning.

- M0's probe on the old renderer: 0 of 276 close vehicles >= 0.50, best 0.170, matched query's top class a vehicle for 8 %, most common top class `chair`.
- First D1 meshes (before the lamp and far-plane fixes): best matched-query vehicle score 0.720 (car), 0.577 (truck); top class a vehicle for 54 %; most common top classes car 48, traffic light 33 (glowing tail lamps), truck 27.
- Lamp emissive was then reduced. Lighting variants (ambient/key/AO) were run as paired dev captures; in-scope car recall was 9/41 and 10/39 for two variants, no measurable difference at that sample size, so the final values follow the spec's direction at a moderate setting rather than being tuned.
- The gate itself: car recall 22/147 = 0.150 in scope. Semantics improved a lot; it is nowhere near 0.70.

## Method notes and caveats

- Scope: `visible` (no building in the way), `agent_occlusion` < 0.5, `extent_from_truth`, class and range per set. `agent_occlusion` is new in this PR (`perception/capture.py`): the share of a box covered by nearer agents' boxes. The existing `visible` flag models buildings only; without it, dense-traffic frames charged recall for cars hidden behind a lead vehicle (41 in-scope cars with it, 153 without, on one dev capture).
- Not modelled, so denominators are inflated and recall is understated for people in particular: tree trunks and canopies, poles and signs. Crops of hazard labels show several boxes on empty street or behind a trunk. The car verdict does not depend on this; read the pedestrian and cyclist rows as "at most" until tree/pole occluders are added.
- Hazard precision is 0.000 with 72 false positives: 56 pedestrian-class detections, many on cyclist riders (counted wrong by per-class matching); 15 correct detections matched out-of-scope labels and are ignored.
- Position error is detector-attributable (matched detection box vs label box through the same ground projection); n = 34 within 20 m and n = 1 beyond, so the 20-40 m figure is not meaningful. End-to-end error against the agent centre is not scored: the published position is a near-face contact point.
- fp32 was not scored and no weights were downloaded (the 82.6 MB fp32 file is not cached), so the fp32-vs-int8 latency rule was not exercised; Gate 1 is int8 only.
- The frozen continuity sets (`contract/benchmark`, `benchmark-v2`; old 640x384 renderer) score 0 detections at 0.50, matching Cycle 5.
- The machine was saturated (load average 150-550) during the Nob Hill attempt, which produced 0 labelled frames in 25 minutes (labelling drops frames that lag past the pose-history window). Retry on a quiet machine with `EXTRA_SERVE_ARGS="--tick-hz 3 --source osm"` and `scripts/run_capture.sh osm-nob-hill <seed> - <dir>` (`-` now omits `--traffic`), then `scripts/build_d1_benchmark.py --name benchmark-nobhill --keep any ...` and add it to `SETS` in `tests/test_benchmark_d1.py`.
- Selection: frames with a stopped ego (pose within 0.3 m / 0.02 rad of the last kept frame) are dropped; `benchmark-close` keeps frames with a vehicle label, `benchmark-hazards` frames with a pedestrian or cyclist label (recorded in the manifests).
