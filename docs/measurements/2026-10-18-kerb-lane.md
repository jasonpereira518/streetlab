# Right turns from the kerb lane

**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Branch:** `claude/kerb-lane` (phase 7c of the realism plan, stacked on 7b,
PR #64)

## What was wrong

On a street with two lanes each way the ego drove the INNER lane and turned
right from it, across the kerb lane and through anything driving or waiting
there. Traffic yielded to it (phase 5), but no driver turns right from the
inner lane.
- **Why:** a half-lane inset from the centreline (`EGO_LANE_INSET`, OSM's
  `lane_inset`) is the inner lane wherever the centreline is the divider.
- **The grid:** Hyde and California St, on grid-loop, grid-signals,
  grid-merge and grid-overtake.
- **Nob Hill:** the loop's 440 m along California Street (2+2), which ends in
  a right turn.

## What it does now

- **The grid:** every leg of a loop is driven in its kerb lane:
  `street.half_width - LANE_W / 2`. Every corner of a clockwise loop is a
  right turn, from the kerb lane into the kerb lane. One-lane streets are
  unchanged (1.8 m), and the boulevard was already there.
- **OSM:** each run of straight legs takes the lane its run ends turning
  from: the kerb lane into a right turn, the inner lane into a left
  (`_turns_right_next`; `lane_inset(road, kerb=True)` is the rightmost driven
  lane, beside any parking or bus lane). It differs from before only on
  two-way streets with two or more lanes each way.
- **Overtaking:** from the kerb lane the lane beside is to the LEFT, so every
  admitted lane change, the ego's and traffic's, is now a pass on the left
  and a return to the right. Before, every one was a pass on the right.

| Loop | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| grid-loop, grid-signals, grid-merge | 295.2 m | 280.8 m |
| grid-overtake | 455.2 m | 448.0 m |
| grid-arterial, grid-night (one-lane streets) | 615.2 m | 615.2 m |
| Nob Hill, along California Street | 1.8 m from the centreline | 5.4 m (its kerb lane) |

## Found on the way

Moving the ego's lane moved where traffic drives and when the ego meets it.
Dense traffic is chaotic, so each fix reshuffled it and surfaced the next
latent defect. Six, five of them fixed:

- **Ambient lanes over the pavement (fixed).** A chain of same-name ways was
  driven with its FIRST way's lane layout. Nob Hill's California Street chain
  starts on a 2+2 way, so it carried a lane 5.4 m out; where the street
  narrows to 1+1 that lane ran 1.8 m over the pavement, and a dense-traffic
  car drove 3.0 m over the kerb (seed 7, t = 24.5 s).
  - **Fix:** a chain carries the fewest lanes each way of any way in it. This
    retires the `ponytail:` note it replaces.
  - **Test:** every Nob Hill ambient lane centre stays on its carriageway.
    With the old layout it is 1.80 m off.
- **A rear corner swinging into a stopped truck (fixed).** A loop car stopped
  at a red 0.87 m out of its lane, mid-change. When it resumed at the full 15°
  crab, its rear corner swung 0.03 m into a truck stopped in the next lane
  (dense grid-merge, seed 7).
  - **Fix:** the slide angle is now also bounded by the room beside the rear
    half on the side it swings to (`IdmTraffic._slide_tan`), with a 3° floor
    so a slide always finishes. It resumes at 8°, 0.25 m clear.
- **A car coming round into a turning truck (fixed).** A car at its street's
  end came round to the street's start, which was clear by `_lane_taken`.
  That check only counts cars whose centres are in the lane, so it missed a
  truck standing there mid-turn on a turn route. The car came round 2.55 m
  deep into it (dense Nob Hill, seed 7, t = 169.6 s).
  - **Fix:** the respawn pose must also keep `_AMBIENT_SPAWN_CLEAR_M` from
    every nearby body.
  - **Its margin:** first set to the spawn clearance (3 m), it held cars at
    their street's end for good. The car in the opposite lane beside the
    street's start is 1.7 m off, so 12 cars never came round. It is 0.5 m:
    enough not to land on a body.
- **Trucks in lanes too narrow for them (fixed).** Broadway's last block
  before the tunnel is a 3.0 m one-way lane, narrowed by the buildings either
  side. A 2.4 m truck following it round into the junction swung 0.13 m over
  the kerb.
  - **Fix:** ambient lanes carry their width (a chain its narrowest way's).
    A truck is spawned only in a lane with 0.4 m to spare each side, else a
    car takes its slot, and a street-end turn skips a lane its vehicle does
    not fit.
- **Trucks on street-end turns (fixed).** The turn route's fillet is trimmed
  to half its shorter leg, and the leg onto the new lane is 6 m, so the
  corner actually driven is ~3 m. A 7.8 m truck cut the kerb by 0.03 m on one
  (Polk at Jackson).
  - **Fix:** a body longer than that leg gets a leg its own length, and a
    rounder corner. Cars' turns are unchanged.
- **Red-light runner on grid at 0.45x traffic (recorded, not fixed).** The
  ego sees it, brakes and is nearly stopped under AEB in 3 of 3 seeds. On
  seed 1 the re-timed runner still crawls into its nose (−0.54 m).
  - **Why:** from the kerb lane the ego is on the side the runner comes from,
    so they meet sooner.
  - **Status:** the same documented gap as the normal-speed grid cell (stop
    short of a conflict already seen; phase 8 planner work). It is added to
    `KNOWN_GAPS`, the one entry this phase adds.

## Expectations that moved with it

- **Grid-loop's starting lane:** index 1 of 2 (kerb), with lane-line paint on
  its left.
- **Admitted lane changes:** to the left (+1) on grid-loop (2) and Nob Hill
  (12), not the right.
- **Traffic lane-change tests** (`test_mobil.py`) target the inner lane.
- **Nob Hill's voluntary turn-back test** has something to judge again (a pass
  on the left that holds the lane), so its strict xfail is removed.
- **Grid-loop at 0.45x:**
  - **Budget run:** recorded for 460 s (was 400 s). The first stop behind a
    stopped lead is now at 434–449 s, at a 2.68 m gap, inside the 2–4 m
    budget.
  - **Yellow onset:** one stop begins as a light turns yellow with the ego at
    4.5 m/s and brakes at 2.74 m/s². It is recorded with Nob Hill's
    yellow-onset stops.
- **Grid red-light runner:** its seed-2 overlap no longer happens, so it
  leaves `KNOWN_OVERLAPS`.
- **Oncoming drift:** the staged car's route now follows the centre line
  station by station. One offset worked out at a single station put it 3.5 m
  over the line where the ego's offset changed between streets.
- **A phantom brake** now means farther than 10 m AND more than 5 s away at
  the closing speed. Measured on grid-merge: the ego yields to a loop car
  moving back into its lane 15.3 m ahead and 3.7 m/s slower, which is real.
